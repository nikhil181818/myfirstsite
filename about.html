Jaipur Engineering College (JEC) Kukas, established in 2000, is a premier institution in Rajasthan, affiliated with Rajasthan Technical University (RTU), Kota. The college offers a wide range of B.Tech and M.Tech programs in various engineering disciplines, focusing on emerging technologies like Artificial Intelligence and Data Science. JEC is known for its state-of-the-art infrastructure, including modern laboratories, a central library, and a vibrant campus life. The college also boasts a dedicated placement cell that facilitates internships and job opportunities with top companies, ensuring students receive comprehensive support for their career development.
